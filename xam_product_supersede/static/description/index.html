Product Supersession & Replacement Management

Manage product supersession, replacement chains, and interchangeable products with strong validations, effective dates, and a seamless product form experience.

Quality Odoo 17+ view compliant & ORM optimized

Key Features

  • ✔ Relationship types: Supersedes, Superseded By, Interchangeable.
  • ✔ Automatic reverse relationship creation and synchronization.
  • ✔ Prevent circular supersession loops (A → B → A).
  • ✔ Validity control with Effective Date and Expiration Date.
  • ✔ Computed status: Future / Active / Expired.
  • ✔ Product form integration: dedicated tab + “Superseded” ribbon.
  • ✔ One-click replacement in draft/sent quotations (when Sales is installed).
  • ✔ Chatter tracking for auditability via mail.thread.

Typical Use Cases

  • SKU replacement and new product versions
  • Engineering revision control
  • Supplier changes and discontinued products
  • Inventory modernization & data governance

Technical Notes

  • Compatible with Odoo 17+ view rules (no legacy attrs/states).
  • Multi-company aware relationships.
  • Sales integration is optional and safely guarded.
